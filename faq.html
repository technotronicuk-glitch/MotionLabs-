Frequent Inquiries

How does the 'Neural Sync' fitting work? +
The fitting happens in two stages at our London lab. First, we map your peripheral nerve signals using bio-sensors. Second, our AI calibrates the limb to match your unique gait or grip pressure.
Is the OcuScan eye available for clinical use? +
OcuScan is currently in Phase II Research. It is not yet available for general sale, but we are accepting participants for UK-based clinical trials in late 2026.
